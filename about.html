About Thaghrah

Why Practical Cybersecurity Learning Matters

From theory-heavy lessons to hands-on packet analysis that builds real skills.

Objectives

Hands-on packet analysis

Deliver a web-based learning environment where beginners develop practical Wireshark and PCAP investigation skills through structured, repeatable challenges.

Structured progression

Implement sequential unlocks, scoring, badge milestones, and flag validation so learners advance with clear feedback and measurable progress.

AI-assisted practice

Integrate an AI Lab pipeline that generates investigation scenarios and downloadable PCAPs to diversify practice beyond fixed challenge banks.

Technical validation

Build and evaluate a deployable Flask–SQLite–Scapy stack that demonstrates feasibility for classroom use and future iteration by peers or instructors.

The Problem

Theory vs Practice

Students may understand networking protocols academically but struggle to apply this knowledge during real packet investigations.

Wireshark Complexity

Filtering traffic, following streams, and identifying suspicious packets in Wireshark can overwhelm beginner learners.

Limited Practice

Many cybersecurity learning environments lack structured progression and repeatable packet-analysis scenarios for continuous hands-on practice.

Why Thaghrah Matters

  • Bridges the theory-practice gap in cybersecurity learning.
  • Provides a safe offline environment for packet analysis.
  • Builds confidence in Wireshark through structured repetition.
  • Supports scalable practice via AI-generated scenarios.
  • Improves retention using progressive challenge design.

Project Overview

Thaghrah is an interactive cybersecurity learning platform that teaches Wireshark and network analysis through hands-on challenges, gamified labs, and AI-generated cybersecurity scenarios. Learn, practice, and level up your cyber skills in a fun and practical way.

Conclusion & future work

Conclusion

  • The evaluation confirmed that the implemented platform operated successfully within the intended educational environment.
  • The system successfully supported core functionalities including structured challenge progression, flag validation, downloadable PCAP generation, security protections, performance responsiveness, and AI-generated challenge creation.
  • The platform effectively delivered guided packet-analysis learning through practical forensic investigation exercises, helping bridge the gap between theoretical networking knowledge and hands-on cybersecurity practice.

Future Work

  • Expand challenge categories beyond network traffic analysis to cover broader cybersecurity domains.
  • Improve the consistency, realism, and reliability of AI-generated challenge scenarios.
  • Enhance gamification features by introducing team-based competition elements, such as leaderboards and collaborative challenge participation, to encourage engagement beyond individual users.
  • Introduce real-time packet capture and live traffic analysis alongside downloadable PCAP-based workflows.
